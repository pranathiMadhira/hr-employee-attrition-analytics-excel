# HR Employee Attrition Analytics — Excel

## Project Overview

An end-to-end HR analytics project built in Microsoft Excel to analyze employee attrition and identify workforce retention risks.

## Business Problem

Employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee data to answer:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which job roles are most affected?
- Which age groups have higher attrition?
- Does overtime relate to employee attrition?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- Which job roles show higher attrition among overtime employees?

## Dataset

- 1,470 employee records

## Key Findings

- Overall employee attrition is **16.12%**.
- Sales has the highest department attrition at **21.10%**.
- Sales Representatives have the highest job-role attrition at **37.96%**.
- Employees under 25 have the highest age-band attrition at **38.82%**.
- Employees working overtime have **29.55%** attrition compared with **10.67%** for employees without overtime.
- Low job satisfaction has **23.44%** attrition compared with **11.32%** for very high satisfaction.
- Among overtime employees, Research Scientists have the highest attrition at **33.22%**.

## Recommendations

- Review overtime and workload distribution.
- Investigate retention challenges in high-risk job roles.
- Strengthen early-career mentoring and development.
- Improve employee engagement and job satisfaction.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-risk departments and roles.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard summarizes key HR KPIs and attrition patterns across department, job role, age, overtime, job satisfaction, and overtime-related job-role risk.
